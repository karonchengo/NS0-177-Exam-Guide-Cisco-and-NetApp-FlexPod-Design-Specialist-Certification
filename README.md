## What Is the NS0-177 Cisco and NetApp FlexPod Design Specialist Exam?

The NS0-177 exam is associated with the Cisco and NetApp FlexPod Design Specialist certification. It assesses a candidate's ability to understand FlexPod hardware components and select appropriate configurations based on customer requirements.

FlexPod combines Cisco computing and networking technologies with NetApp storage to provide an integrated infrastructure platform for business-critical applications. The certification focuses on designing these solutions to meet workload requirements while helping organizations manage infrastructure complexity, cost, and risk.

Unlike a general networking or storage certification, NS0-177 emphasizes the architectural decisions involved in selecting and sizing FlexPod components, configuring networking, addressing security requirements, and identifying the appropriate tools for managing and troubleshooting solutions.

## Who Should Take the NS0-177 Exam?

The certification is suitable for IT professionals involved in infrastructure architecture, solution design, and enterprise technology planning.

Typical candidates include:
- Infrastructure and Solution Architects
- Data Center Engineers
- Network Architects and Engineers
- Storage Architects and Administrators

NetApp recommends six to twelve months of field experience with the tools and standards used to assess FlexPod computing solution performance and requirements. Candidates should also understand the hardware components used in FlexPod and how to select suitable hardware for specific workloads.
